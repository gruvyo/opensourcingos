# Project classification defaults

Project Type distinguishes commercial Sourcing projects from non-commercial Support requests. Within each project type, Type answers why the work exists and Status describes its workflow stage.

Sourcing Event Type must not duplicate savings strategy or value levers. Baselines, offers, awards, and savings calculations remain the authoritative commercial chain.

## Sourcing

Types, in display order:

1. Contract Renewal
2. New Purchase
3. Mid-Contract Commercial Review
4. Demand or Specification Change
5. Supplier or Market Change
6. Other Sourcing Initiative

Statuses, in display order:

1. Pipeline
2. Scoping & Strategy
3. In Market
4. Negotiation
5. Award & Contracting
6. Implementation
7. Complete
8. On Hold
9. Cancelled

## Support

Types, in display order:

1. Vendor Performance or Service Issue
2. Billing or Payment Issue
3. Contract Inquiry
4. Operational Request
5. Risk or Compliance Review
6. General Inquiry

Statuses, in display order:

1. Not Started
2. In Progress
3. Pending
4. Complete
5. Cancelled

These are editable workspace choices. During the public beta, the August 2026 taxonomy reset reclassified existing fictional and beta workspace projects and removed the original ideation defaults. The migration leaves relational identifiers and every savings record unchanged, and the existing workspace audit trail records the managed-choice changes.
